🇬🇧United Kingdom

Europe · 2002 – 2005

Overview

The United Kingdom was home for three years during a period of professional training and European Computer Driving Licence certification. Time there was split between London and the borough of Tottenham in North London, where work in IT infrastructure and freelance web development occupied most of the schedule.

The country offered a different pace and a window into European engineering practice, data centre operations, and the early culture of co-location hosting at a time when internet infrastructure was still taking its current shape.

Cities

London

London anchored the UK years. A city that moves constantly, it was a place of professional firsts: first European certification, first exposure to large-scale IT infrastructure, and first experience of a working culture built around precision and accountability. The city itself repaid every spare moment given to it.

Tottenham, North London

Tottenham was the base of operations during the UK years. Work at The Technology Centre here produced the ECDL qualification and a practical grounding in web programming. North London at that time was a place of visible contrast: working-class energy, a strong West African community, and a pace of life that felt both familiar and foreign.
