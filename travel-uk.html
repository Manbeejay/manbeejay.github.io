🇬🇧United Kingdom

Europe · 2002 – 2005

Overview

Three years in the United Kingdom left a mark that has not faded. The base was London and Tottenham, where work at Redbus Interhouse and study at The Technology Centre occupied most of the schedule. But the country itself kept pulling outward. Leicester, Liverpool, Manchester, Southampton, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow — each city had its own character and left its own memory.

Britain is a place that rewards curiosity. Every train journey opened onto something unexpected, and most of those experiences have stayed vivid for more than two decades.

London

London was the anchor. A city that runs on speed and ambition, it was the setting for professional firsts: work inside a European co-location data centre, exposure to large-scale IT infrastructure, and a daily routine built in one of the world's busiest capitals. Every spare hour was spent exploring.

Liverpool

A road trip with a friend to a Volkswagen Beetle festival somewhere near Liverpool. People came from across Europe to display every imaginable variation on the VW Beetle — original classics, modified builds, rare editions. The atmosphere was part car show, part street carnival, and entirely unexpected.
