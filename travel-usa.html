North America · 2007

Overview

America spread across several cities and a range of experiences. Houston came first for engineering project development training at RWD Technologies University. New York, New Jersey, and Chicago filled out the picture — each city a different side of the same vast country.

The United States runs on a scale that takes time to absorb. The skylines, the distances, the energy of cities that never fully stop. Each visit left something different behind.

Houston, Texas

Houston is an engineering city. Oil and gas infrastructure everywhere, a professional culture built on technical precision, and a skyline that exists because of petroleum. RWD Technologies University ran intensive project development sessions using real Chevron data sets. The training fed directly into subsequent work on Chevron Nigeria's Operations, Maintenance and Training manuals.
